Data & references
Emission factor
0.710 kg CO₂/kWh — CEA CO₂ Baseline Database v21.0, FY 2024-25. National grid, not Tamil Nadu.
Tariff — gross (₹/unit)
0–400: 4.95 · 401–500: 6.65 · 501–600: 8.80 · 601–800: 9.95 · 801–1000: 11.05 · above 1000: 12.15. Slabs are bi-monthly.
Tariff model
Billing is telescopic: each band is charged at its own rate, so a saving is worth the rate at the top of your consumption, not a blended average. The bench prices it as bill(units) − bill(units − saving), which stays correct when a saving straddles a band. Above 500 units the subsidised schedule falls back to the gross bands.
Tariff — net after TN subsidy
0–100: free · 101–200: 2.35 or 2.60 · 201–400: 4.70 · 401–500: 6.30. Above 500 the subsidy tapers out.
Tariff — institutional
Govt educational/hospital 8.80 · private educational/hostel 9.40. TANGEDCO LT-IA and LT-IIB, TNERC suo motu CPI order effective 1 July 2025.
Load factor
Nameplate is an upper bound, and the two units derate differently. A 19-inch CRT rated 120 W measures about 50 W in use, hence 50–70% for displays; an LED monitor sits closer to its rating, and a laptop's adapter is rated well above what the machine draws. Preset pairs are typical values — measure and overwrite them.
Standby
Counted over the hours the unit is idle but still plugged in, i.e. 24 − (hours connected × duty). Preset values are typical, not readings; enter 0 if the appliance is switched off at the socket. A CRT at 3 W for 18 h/day is about 20 kWh a year.
Embodied share
Embodied carbon is derived from the new unit's own operating figures as operating × s/(1−s). 81% for computers and CRT displays is Williams (2004); 18% for lighting is the complement of the ~82% use-phase share quoted for CFLs. The 12% used for motors and 20% for custom pairs are assumptions, not citations — replace them with a manufacturer or LCA figure.
Lamp equivalence
At ~800 lm: 60 W incandescent ≈ 14 W CFL ≈ 9 W LED (ENERGY STAR). Unmatched wattages overstate the saving.
Method
ISO 50002 Level 1–2, IS 13979. Table cells are labelled measured, derived or typical. Payback is simple and undiscounted. Inputs and banked pairs are stored in this browser only — nothing is uploaded. Append ?selftest to the URL to run the built-in arithmetic checks in the console.
References
Williams (2004) Environ. Sci. Technol. 38(22) — 81% of a computer's lifetime energy is embodied. Kim, Keoleian & Horie (2006) Energy Policy 34(15). Bhakar et al. (2015) Procedia CIRP 29.
Disposal
CRT glass 1.8–3.6 kg lead; CFLs contain mercury. CPCB-authorised recycler, E-Waste (Management) Rules 2022.
