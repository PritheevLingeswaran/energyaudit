Data & references
Emission factor
0.710 kg CO₂/kWh — CEA CO₂ Baseline Database v21.0, FY 2024-25. National grid average, not Tamil Nadu alone.
Electricity rate
One flat ₹ per unit, entered by you. Slab tariffs charge each band at its own rate, so use the rate of the highest band your bill reaches — that is what a saved unit is worth.
Load factor
Nameplate is an upper bound, and the two units derate differently. The preset wattages, load factors and power factors are typical values, not readings — measure and overwrite them.
Lamp equivalence
At ~800 lm: 14 W CFL ≈ 9 W LED (ENERGY STAR). Unmatched wattages overstate the saving.
Method
Every table cell is labelled measured, derived, typical or calculated. Payback is simple and undiscounted. Inputs are stored in this browser only — nothing is uploaded.
Disposal
A CRT holds roughly 2–4 kg of lead in its glass; CFLs contain mercury. Hand replaced units to a CPCB-registered recycler under the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 — not to household waste.
